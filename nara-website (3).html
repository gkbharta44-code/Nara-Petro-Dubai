Procurement procedure

Our process

Current working procedures for procurement of petroleum products, as described in the company profile.

Buyer intent & offer

NARA sends an FCO / Proforma Offer after receiving the buyer’s intent to purchase.

Acceptance

The buyer accepts the offer by sending an acceptance letter on official letterhead.

Draft SPA

NARA sends a draft SPA for completion and mutual agreement.

Letter of credit

The buyer issues an irrevocable, documentary, transferable letter of credit covering the product value.

Bank confirmation

NARA’s bank confirms acceptance of the payment instrument issued by the buyer’s bank.

Delivery planning

Within 24 hours of LC confirmation, NARA finalizes the delivery plan with the refinery.

Insurance & shipment

The product is insured and paid for by NARA or the end buyer, according to the agreement, as shipment is confirmed.

Arrival & inspection

The vessel arrives at the buyer’s designated discharge port and quantity and quality are confirmed.

Final payment

NARA draws final payment from the LC according to the final invoice after delivery.
